Plinko Tycoon – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Plinko Tycoon ("the app") is developed by Parker Swanson ("we"). This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (coins, upgrades, settings) is saved locally on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (including the Identifier for Advertisers, IDFA, if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalize ads. On iOS you will be asked for permission before the IDFA is used, and you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Users in regions that require it are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and restore them across devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history; it does not receive your name or payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your Game Center display name and scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards and are visible to other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders (for example, when your offline vault is full). These are created on your device and no data is sent to us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can decline ad tracking, disable notifications, or delete the app at any time, which removes all locally stored data.
